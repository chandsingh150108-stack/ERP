## 4. Student Web Access & Directory Features

Students accessing `http://localhost:8080/faculty` benefit from:
1. **Dean to Assistant Professor Hierarchy**:
   - Visual color-coded cards and badges (Gold for Deans, Purple for HODs, Green for Professors, Cyan for Assistant Professors).
2. **Instant Contact Accessibility**:
   - Clickable phone numbers (`tel:+91...`) and email addresses (`mailto:...`) for direct communication.
3. **Teaching Allocation Transparency**:
   - Every faculty card details their exact **2 assigned subjects** with course codes and subject titles.
4. **Real-time Filter & Search**:
   - Search across name, subject code, title, email, phone number, and employee ID.
   - Filter by rank (Deans & Admins, HODs, Professors, Assistant Professors) or by department.
5. **Vault Aesthetic**:
   - Matches the dark theme, responsive design, and profile modal integrated with the student ERP.
